# Annotation and Review Guidelines

These guidelines were used to write the seed counter-narratives and to review
every generated pair in rounds V2–V5.

## Record format

Each record has an `ID`, the `Text`, a `Category` (`Homophobic` or
`Non-Homophobic`), a `Target` and a `Counter Narrative`. Non-homophobic
comments that mention LGBTQ+ identities form a control class, so that models
do not treat every mention of LGBTQ+ people as hateful.

## Counter-narrative guidelines

1. **Address the specific claim.** Respond to what the post actually says,
   not with a general statement.
2. **Stay respectful**, whatever the tone of the post.
3. **Never repeat slurs** or add to the hostility.
4. **Keep facts verifiable.** Do not invent statistics, laws or studies.
5. **Match the script and register.** A Romanized Hinglish post gets a
   Romanized Hinglish reply; a Devanagari post may get a Devanagari reply.

## Review procedure (per generated pair)

Check, in order:

- the naturalness of the Hindi–Hinglish;
- the `Category` and `Target` labels;
- that the text is original (not a copy of the seed or an earlier pair);
- abusive wording in the counter-narrative;
- the relevance and safety of the counter-narrative.

Then either **accept** the pair unchanged or **post-edit** the text, the
counter-narrative or both. Keep the generated version next to the edited
version so that editing effort (HTER) can be computed.

## Common reasons for editing

- unnatural wording or spelling;
- a weak link between the claim and the reply;
- wrong or unclear labels;
- repeated reply patterns;
- replies that were unsafe or more confrontational than needed.
